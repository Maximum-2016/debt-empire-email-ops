# 10-Month Nurture Calendar — Debt Empire

**Wall:** MGP · **Primary CTA arc:** awareness → clarity → fit → booked discovery/enrollment call  
**Cadence:** ~2–4 emails per lead per month on nurture (lighter on other modes)  
**Rotation:** Brands change across touches so it does not feel like one inbox hammer

Tokens: `{{booking_url}}` · `{{brand_url}}` · UTM via architecture pattern

---

## Cadence rules

| Mode | Typical cadence | Notes |
|------|-----------------|-------|
| Nurture | 2–4 / month | Never more than 1 / ~5–7 days |
| Client-edu | ~1 / week for 6 touches | Pause if HELIX marks sensitive |
| Reengage | 5 over ~3–4 weeks | Then stop or recycle annually |
| Partner | 5 over ~5–6 weeks | Then monthly digest (future) |

---

## CTA progression (nurture)

| Phase | Months | CTA emphasis |
|-------|--------|--------------|
| Orient | 1–2 | Reply / read / soft “learn more” at `{{brand_url}}` |
| Clarify | 3–4 | Optional clarity / pressure-map call |
| Fit | 5–7 | Discovery call — “see if settlement is a fit” |
| Commit | 8–10 | Enrollment-oriented booking; scarcity avoided — urgency from *their* ACH pain only |

## Month-by-month themes
