When cash is tight, the loudest offer is usually another advance — hard-money style, same-day funding, “we’ll work with your current stack.”

**Advance Alternatives Brief** exists to slow that moment down. We don’t lend. We don’t sell a product in this note. We help you ask better questions before you sign.

**Three checks before any new capital:**
1. Does this reduce your daily/weekly pulls — or add a fifth one?
2. What’s the all-in cost vs. status quo for 90 days? (Illustrative math only.)
3. If nothing changes operationally, will this buy time or buy a deeper hole?

Clarity first. Commitment later.
