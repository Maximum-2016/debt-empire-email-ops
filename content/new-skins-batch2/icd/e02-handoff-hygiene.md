Hi {{first_name}},

When you refer a distressed merchant into Debt Empire’s settlement / education lane, **wording matters**.

**Helpful:**
- “I’m connecting you with a team that reviews stacked ACH situations.”
- “They’ll look at fit — no guarantee of outcome.”

**Avoid:**
- Promising wipeouts, specific settlement %, or legal results.
- Presenting a settlement desk as “the same company as your funder.”
- Implying an attorney relationship unless counsel is actually engaged.

ISO Care Desk exists so partners don’t have to improvise that language under pressure.
