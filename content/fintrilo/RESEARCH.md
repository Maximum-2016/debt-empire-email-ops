## Positioning

- **One-line:** Unified business finance partner — lending + merchant processing (+ debt/financial optimization) under one consultative roof.  
- **Name story (public):** Fin = finance; Tri = three solutions / three founders; Lo = **Liaison Officer**.  
- **Differentiator:** Owners shouldn’t juggle separate vendors for funding vs payments; Liaison Officers “listen, analyze, and guide — not push products.”  
- **Audience:** US SMB owners (all 50 states language on site) — restaurants, retail, service, convenience, etc.  
- **Secondary audience (MGP ops):** ISO / sales partners overlapping Biz-Pay style merchant acquisition — **MGP wall**, not FM.
